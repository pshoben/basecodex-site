How it works

Listening first

Every lesson is spoken by native voices. You hear a sentence before you read it, and ear training works on the sounds English speakers tend to merge.

A plan for every day

Each day is a short set: listen, learn, recall, fix. Reviews that are due come before anything new, so missing a day never turns into a backlog.

Today, in French

A fresh news edition every morning, written for learners from stories confirmed by independent sources.

Learn together

Invite family and friends into a circle and learn alongside each other. Free to start, with a subscription for the full course.

GCSE French

A listening course built around the exam

A GCSE mode for the CCEA French listening paper follows the board’s Foundation vocabulary list day by day, over 180 days, until every word on it has been heard in context. Practice questions are derived from real past papers, each checked by a person, and a mock exam runs on the board’s own clock.
