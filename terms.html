2. Subscriptions and free trials

  • What you can use for free. You can start the course without a subscription. A subscription unlocks the rest of the app.
  • Who sells it. Podyglot Premium is an auto-renewing monthly subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple takes the payment and keeps your payment details. We never see them.
  • Prices. The standard price and the lower circle price (section 3) are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy, in your own currency. The price includes any sales tax Apple charges.
  • Free trial. New subscribers can start with a free trial, once per Apple Account. It turns into a paid subscription at the end unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
  • Renewal. The subscription renews each month at the price of your plan unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Apple charges your Apple Account within the 24 hours before each renewal.
  • Managing and cancelling. In the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its own terms (reportaproblem.apple.com). We cannot give refunds ourselves.
  • Family Sharing. If the person who pays has Family Sharing turned on, the members of their Apple family group can use the subscription too.

3. Invites, circles, gift tokens and free periods

These features have rules, set out in the app where you use them. In short:

  • Invites. An invite is a one-use code for one friend. It expires after three days. A household can have its free month from an invite once, ever.
  • Circles. The circle price applies while someone in another household in your circle is paying. If your circle stops qualifying, the app tells you and asks you to switch to the standard price or invite friends. Apple never changes your price by itself.
  • Gift tokens and certificates. Gift tokens are earned by paying and learning, and can be given to other learners. Five received tokens make a certificate for a free month. Tokens and certificates have no cash value, cannot be sold or exchanged outside Podyglot, and expire after the time the app shows.
  • Codes from the Podyglot team (for testers, teachers and others) give the free period they say, on the terms we gave them out on.
  • Free periods from any of these are given by Apple as offers on your subscription, under Apple's terms.

We may cancel an invite, code, token or free period that was obtained or used against these terms.

4. Podyglot names, classes and family boards

  • Your Podyglot name (your avatar) is chosen from word lists and a number. It is the only name other learners see, and it is never given to anyone else.
  • Classes. A teacher with a teacher code can set up a class, and pupils ask to join it. Teachers who use Podyglot with a class are responsible for doing so under their school's own rules.
  • Family boards. The person who pays can open a family board and let people join it. Everyone on a board sees each member's Podyglot name, level and a summary of their progress. Anyone can leave at any time. Being on a board does not share a subscription.
